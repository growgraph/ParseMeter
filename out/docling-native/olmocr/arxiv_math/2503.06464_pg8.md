• Labeled graphs. Denote by Kn the complete graph with vertex set [n] and edge set Un. For

any graph H, let V (H) denote the vertex set of H and let E(H) denote the edge set of H.

We say H is a subgraph of G, denoted by H ⊂ G, if V (H) ⊂ V (G) and E(H) ⊂ E(G). We

say φ : V (H) → V (S) is an injection, if for all (i, j) ∈ E(H) we have (φ(i), φ(j)) ∈ E(S).

For H, S ⊂ Kn, denote by H ∩ S the graph with vertex set given by V (H) ∩ V (S) and

edge set given by E(H) ∩ E(S), and denote by S ∪ H the graph with vertex set given by

V (H) ∪V (S) and edge set given by E(H) ∪E(S). For any graph H, denote the excess of H

by τ(H) = |E(H)| - |V (H)|. Given u ∈ V (H), define NeiH(u) to be the set of neighbors of u

in H. For two vertices u, v ∈ V (H), we define DistH(u, v) to be their graph distance. Denote

the diameter of a connected graph H by Diam(H) = maxu,v∈V (H) DistH(u, v).

• Graph isomorphisms and unlabeled graphs. Two graphs H and H′ are isomorphic, denoted by

H ∼= H′ , if there exists a bijection π : V (H) → V (H′ ) such that (π(u), π(v)) ∈ E(H′ ) if and

only if (u, v) ∈ E(H). Denote by H the isomorphism class of graphs; it is customary to refer

to these isomorphic classes as unlabeled graphs. Let Aut(H) be the number of automorphisms

of H (graph isomorphisms to itself). For any graph H, define Fix(H) = {u ∈ V (H) : φ(u) =

u, ∀φ ∈ Aut(H)}.

• Induced subgraphs. For a graph H = (V,E) and a subset A ⊂ V , define HA = (A,EA) to

be the induced subgraph of H in A, where EA = {(u, v) ∈ E : u, v ∈ A}. Also, define

H\A = (V,E\A) to be the subgraph of H obtained by deleting all edges with both endpoints

in A. Note that EA ∪ E\A = E.

• Isolated vertices. For u ∈ V (H), we say u is an isolated vertex of H if there is no edge in

E(H) incident to u. Denote I(H) as the set of isolated vertices of H.

• Paths, self-avoiding paths and non-backtracking paths. We say a subgraph H ⊂ Kn is a path

with endpoints u, v (possibly with u = v), if there exist w1, . . . ,wm ∈ [n] ̸= u, v such that

V (H) = {u, v, w1, . . . , wm} and E(H) = {(u, w1), (w1, w2) . . . , (wm, v)} (we allow the occur-

rence of multiple vertices or edges). We say H is a self-avoiding path if w0, w1, . . . , wm, wm+1

are distinct (where we denote w0 = u and wm+1 = v), and we say H is a non-backtracking

path if wi+1 ̸= wi-1 for 1 ≤ i ≤m. Denote EndP(P) as the set of endpoints of a path P.

• Cycles and independent cycles. We say a subgraph H is an m-cycle if V (H) = {v1, . . . , vm}

and E(H) = {(v1, v2), . . . , (vm-1, vm), (vm, v1)}. For a subgraph K ⊂ H, we say K is an

independent m-cycle of H, if K is an m-cycle and no edge in E(H) \ E(K) is incident to

V (K). Denote Cm(H) as the set of independent m-cycles of H. For H ⊂ S, we define

Cm(S,H) to be the set of independent m-cycles in S whose vertex set is disjoint from V (H).

• Leaves. A vertex u ∈ V (H) is called a leaf of H, if the degree of u in H is 1; denote L(H) as

the set of leaves of H.
