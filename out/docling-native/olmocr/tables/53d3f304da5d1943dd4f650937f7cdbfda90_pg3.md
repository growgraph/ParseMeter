Second, two sets of three 5-s sweeps were recorded with

the magazine parallel to an artificial palate with 62 activated

linguopalatal distance is smaller than 6-7 mm; this distance

condition was not tested in the present experiment since a

mold was needed to create contact activation on the artificial

palate. It is hypothesized that the two unrealistic distance

conditions tested here should provide information about pos-

sible effects in a real speech production setting, provided

they yielded analogous coil distortion values.

from the magazine without affecting the position of the coils

when the former was parallel to the latter.
