the SWCNTs have a significantly larger surface area of 700 m 2 /g but

have a slightly lower maximum fluoride adsorption capacity of

2.4 mg/g. The pore entry diameter limits for the SWCNTs may account

for the relatively low fluoride adsorption capacity of this 700 m 2 /g

material.
