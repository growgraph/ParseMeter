Ex. 5. $y = \sqrt{x^2 - 1}$.

Using VI, with $u = x^{2} - 1$,

$$
\begin{array}{r l} \frac {d y}{d x} & = \frac {d}{d x} (x ^ {2} - 1) ^ {\frac {1}{2}} = \frac {1}{2} (x ^ {2} - 1) ^ {- \frac {1}{2}} \frac {d}{d x} (x ^ {2} - 1) \\ & = \frac {1}{2} (x ^ {2} - 1) ^ {- \frac {1}{2}} (2 x) = \frac {x}{\sqrt {x ^ {2} - 1}}. \end{array}
$$

Ex. 6. $x^{2} + xy - y^{2} = 1$.

We can consider $y$ a function of $x$ determined by the equation. Then

$$
d (x ^ {2}) + d (x y) - d (y ^ {2}) = d (1) = 0,
$$

that is,

$$
2 x d x + x d y + y d x - 2 y d y = 0,
$$

$$
(2 x + y) d x + (x - 2 y) d y = 0.
$$

Consequently,

$$
\frac {d y}{d x} = \frac {2}{2} \frac {x + y}{y - x}.
$$

Ex. 7. $x = t + \frac{1}{t}, y = t - \frac{1}{t}$.

In this case

$$
d x = d t - \frac {d t}{\overline {{t ^ {2}}}}, \quad d y = d t + \frac {d t}{t ^ {2}}.
$$

Consequently,

$$
\frac {d y}{d x} = \frac {1 + \frac {1}{t ^ {2}}}{1 - \frac {1}{t ^ {2}}} = \frac {t ^ {2} + 1}{t ^ {2} - 1}.
$$
