HMPV F protein has also been shown to mediate cell-cell fusion (34) and virus-cell fusion (3) in the absence of the attachment G protein. Interestingly, syncytium formation by the HMPV F protein expressed alone in transfected cells was dependent on low pH (34). It is therefore possible that members of the _Pneumovirinae_ subfamily have evolved distinct mechanisms of F protein activation compared to those of the _Paramyxovirinae_ subfamily of paramyxoviruses.
