$$\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}T a(x)x^{\alpha}d x=0,\quad\forall f\in L_{N_{p}}^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{n})\mathrm{a n d}|\alpha|\leqslant N_{p}.$$

This condition is actually necessary and sufficient for the boundedness of such operators in $H^{p}(\mathbb{R}^{n})$ However, a local version of such cancellation condition on T is too strong in the local setting (for instance, for pseudodifferential operators). The key to find less restrictive necessary and sufficient conditions for the boundedness of inhomogeneous Calderón-Zygmund operators in the local setting, was to consider approximate atoms and molecules. To do this, if a is an atom in $h^{p}(\mathbb{R}^{n})$ , we show that only the conditions imposed on the kernel imply that $T a$ satisfy the size conditions of the molecule. Then,the additional cancellation condition that $T^{*}((\cdot-c)^{\alpha})$ satisfy a local Campanato-type estimate will show the approximate moment condition of the molecule (see [3, Section 5]). On the other hand, if we suppose that $T a$ satisfies the size condition of molecules,then $T^{*}((\cdot-c)^{\alpha})$ must satisfy the local Campanato type conditions, see [4, Theorem 2].In a more general setting, the authors in [4, Theorem 1] also proved the necessity of this local Campanato condition for genereral linear and bounded operators in $h^{p}(\mathbb{R}^{n})$ that maps atoms into pseudo-molecules, a generalization of pre-molecules, that will be defined in Definition 3.
