## HAVE YOUR SAY ON PUBLIC TRANSPORT IN YOUR LOCAL COMMUNITY ADVANCING PERTH'S EASTERN REGION

Seven Public Transport Forums are being held across the region during October and November 2010. These forums aim to engage and encourage the community to become more aware of the vision for increased public transport and the options and delivery modes that exist. You can register by contacting Samantha Robshaw on email samantha.robshaw@emrc.org.au
