II. SYSTEM MODEL


As shown in Fig. 1, we consider a multiple FAs assisted
IDET system, which is comprised of a transmitter, a DR and
an ER. The transmitter is equipped with _Nt_ FAs, each of which
has the same size and parameters without loss of generality.
The DR and ER are both equipped with a single traditional
antenna due to the limited hardware space. We assume that
the transmit antennas are far apart in space and the channels
of different antennas are fully independent, while the channels
among different ports in the same antenna are strongly spatially
correlated. The set of FAs and FA’s port are denoted by _Nt_ =
_{_ 1 _, · · ·_ _, Nt}_ and _N_ = _{_ 1 _, · · ·_ _, N_ _}_, respectively.
