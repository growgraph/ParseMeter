restart of IRJBD and TRJBD basically costs the same for the same l and kmax, fewer
restarts mean higher efficiency. The experiments were performed on an AMD Ryzen
7 5800X CPU with 78 GB RAM and 8 cores using the MATLAB R2024a with the
