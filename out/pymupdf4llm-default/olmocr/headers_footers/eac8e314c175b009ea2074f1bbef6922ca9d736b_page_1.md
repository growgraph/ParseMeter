### **Parking Lot Renovations Planned**

The Town Commission has approved renovations
and new landscaping for two major parking lots in
the downtown Surfside district. The fi rst is the Abbott
lot between 95th and 96th Streets (west of Harding
Avenue behind the CVS and Flanigan’s). The other is
the 94th Street lot (on the southeast corner of 94th and
Harding Avenue).  Contractors Lynx Construction and
Luke’s Landscaping will be working with Public Works
to complete the renovation. The project is scheduled to
begin before the end of August.
### **Streetscape Improvement Takes Next Step**

The plans for the Harding Avenue Streetscape are
now being reviewed by the Florida Department of
Transportation, since Harding Avenue is a state-owned
street. The Town is planning to start the project as soon
as it is approved by the Florida DOT.



Designed by the fi rms of Stantac and Rosenberg
Gradner, the Harding Avenue Streetscape plan will
transform the downtown area with Medjool palms,
colorful tropical shrubs and pocket parks with benches,
pavers and street crossings.
