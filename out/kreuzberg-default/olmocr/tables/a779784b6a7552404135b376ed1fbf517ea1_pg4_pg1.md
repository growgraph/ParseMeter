# On Monday 18th October, approximately 500 members of our Chisholm community gathered to acknowledge

some of the wonderful team and individual sporting performances for 2010. Special guests Ms Shae Bolton (West Coast Fever Netball) and Mr Trent Mitton (Kookaburras gold medalist at the recent Delhi Commonwealth Games) shared the night with our future sporting stars. Both were able to offer some advice and inspiration for all who attended. Congratulations to all our award winners and in particular Justin Skowron who was awarded the "Peter Tonkin" award for the most outstanding Year 12 sports person.
