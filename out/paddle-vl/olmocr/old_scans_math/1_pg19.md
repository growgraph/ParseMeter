Let

 $$ \frac{x^{2}}{(x^{2}+1)(x^{2}+4)}=\frac{\mathrm{A}}{(x^{2}+1)}+\frac{\mathrm{B}}{(x^{2}+4)} $$ 

 $$ x^{2}=\mathbf{A}(x^{2}+4)+\mathbf{B}(x^{2}+1). $$ 

Let  $ x = \sqrt{-1} $, or  $ x^{2} = -1 $,

 $$ \therefore-1=3\mathbf{A},\quad\therefore\mathbf{A}=-\frac{1}{3}, $$ 

 $$ \therefore x^{2}+\frac{1}{3}\left(x^{2}+4\right)=\mathrm{B}\left(x^{2}+1\right), $$ 

 $$ \therefore\frac{4(x^{2}+1)}{3}=\mathrm{B}(x^{2}+1),\therefore\mathrm{B}=\frac{4}{3} $$ 

 $$ \begin{array}{c}\int\frac{x^{2}dx}{\left(x^{2}+1\right)\left(x^{2}+4\right)}=-\frac{1}{3}\int\left\{\frac{dx}{\left(x^{2}+1\right)}-\int\frac{4dx}{\left(x^{2}+4\right)}\right\}\\=\frac{1}{3}\int\left\{\frac{dx}{\left(1+x^{2}\right)}-\frac{dx}{\left(1+\frac{x^{2}}{4}\right)}\right\}\\=\frac{1}{3}\left\{2\tan^{-1}\frac{x}{2}-\tan^{-1}x\right\}.\end{array} $$ 

 $$ \begin{align*}\text{Or thus,}\int&\frac{x^{2}dx}{(x^{2}+1)(x^{2}+4)}=\frac{1}{3}\int\frac{3x^{2}dx}{(x^{2}+1)(x^{2}+4)}\\&=\frac{1}{3}\int\frac{\{4\,x^{2}+4-(x^{2}+4)\}dx}{(x^{2}+1)(x^{2}+4)}\\&=\frac{1}{3}\int\Big{(}\frac{4dx}{(x^{2}+4)}-\frac{dx}{x^{2}+1}\Big{)}\\&=\frac{1}{3}\Big{(}2\tan^{-1}\frac{x}{2}-\tan^{-1}x\Big{)}\end{align*} $$
