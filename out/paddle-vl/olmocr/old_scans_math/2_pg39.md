Ex. 5.  $ y=\sqrt{x^{2}-1} $

Using VI, with $u = x^{2} - 1$,

 $$ \begin{array}{l}\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{d}{dx}\left(x^2-1\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}=\frac{1}{2}\left(x^2-1\right)^{-\frac{1}{2}}\frac{d}{dx}\left(x^2-1\right)\\ \\ =\frac{1}{2}\left(x^2-1\right)^{-\frac{1}{2}}\left(2\ x\right)=\frac{x}{\sqrt{x^2-1}}.\end{array} $$ 

Ex. 6.  $ x^{2} + xy - y^{2} = 1 $.

We can consider y a function of x determined by the equation. Then

 $$ d\left(x^{2}\right)+d\left(xy\right)-d\left(y^{2}\right)=d\left(1\right)=0, $$ 

that is,

 $$ \begin{array}{l}2x dx+x dy+y dx-2y dy=0,\\ (2x+y)dx+(x-2y)dy=0.\end{array} $$ 

Consequently,

 $$ \frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{2x+y}{2y-x}. $$ 

Ex. 7.  $ x = t + \frac{1}{t} $,  $ y = t - \frac{1}{t} $.

In this case

 $$ d x=d t-\frac{d t}{t^{2}},\quad d y=d t+\frac{d t}{t^{2}}. $$ 

Consequently,

 $$ \frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{1+\frac{1}{t^{2}}}{1-\frac{1}{t^{2}}}=\frac{t^{2}+1}{t^{2}-1}. $$
