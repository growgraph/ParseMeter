## CHAPTER I.

ELEMENTARY INTEGRALS TO BE COMMITTED TO MEMORY.

(1.)  $ \int \frac{d x}{x} = \log x. $

(2.)

 $$ \begin{array}{l}\int\frac{d x}{a+b\,x^{2}}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{a\,b}}\tan^{-1}\Big(\,x\,\sqrt{\frac{b}{a}}\Big),\text{or}\int\frac{d x}{a^{2}+x^{2}}\\=\frac{1}{a}\tan^{-1}\frac{x}{a}.\end{array} $$ 

(3.)

 $$ \int\frac{dx}{\sqrt{a^{2}-x^{2}}}=\sin^{-1}\frac{x}{a} $$ 

(4.)

 $$ \int\frac{-dx}{\sqrt{a^{2}-x^{2}}}=\cos^{-1}\frac{x}{a} $$ 

(5.)

 $$ \int\frac{dx}{\sqrt{x^{2}\pm a^{2}}}=\log\frac{(x+\sqrt{x^{2}\pm a^{2}})}{a} $$ 

(6.)

 $$ \int\frac{dx}{\sqrt{2ax-x^{2}}}=\operatorname{vers}^{-1}\frac{x}{a} $$ 

(7.)

 $$ \int\frac{dx}{\sqrt{x^{2}+2ax}}=\log(x\pm a+\sqrt{x^{2}+2ax}) $$ 

(8.)

 $$ \int\frac{dx}{x\sqrt{x^{2}-a^{2}}}=\frac{1}{a}\sec^{-1}\frac{x}{a} $$
